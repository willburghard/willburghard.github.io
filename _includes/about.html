About

Welcome to my website! My name is Will Burghard and I am an Audio Visual Technician, Programmer, and Musician based in New York City. I built this website using Github Pages, Jekyll, and Bootstrap to showcase some of my professional and personal projects. I worked as an Audio Visual Technician from 2022-2024 at Southridge Audio Productions. Since October 2024, I have worked as a freelance AV professional and as a part-time IT technician at Eve Lom. As a freelance AV technician, I have engineered audio and video for such companies as Five Star AV and the Maxus Group, and have worked events at such venues as the Virgin Hotel on Broadway and the NYU School of Law. At Eve Lom, I provide crucial IT support to employees, including setting up new laptops for employees, wiping laptops from old employees, troubleshooting issues with software and hardware, and authoring detailed spreadsheets on forecast data in Microsoft Excel. With regards to programming, I am an AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and have extensive experience with several programming languages including C++, Java, JavaScript, HTML, and SQL, as well as a working knowledge of several others, including C# and Haskell. I have a BA in Computer Science from Bard College. You can find a compilation of my various programming projects by navigating to the "Portfolio" section of this website. With regards to music, I play in an alternative rock band called "Retracer" and make ambient/experimental music under the moniker "World's Museum." I hope you have fun exploring my website!
