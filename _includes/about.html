About

Welcome to my website! My name is Will Burghard and I am a dedicated and passionate professional based in New York City with a diverse range of experience in technology including IT, software engineering, and audiovisual technology. I built this website using Github Pages, Jekyll, and Bootstrap to showcase some of my professional and personal projects.
After graduating from Bard College with a degree in Computer Science, I began my career in November 2020 working as a software developer at the broadcast analytics company Lake 5 Media, where I built 18 different microservice applications for their SaaS suites that large advertising companies such as ABC, NBC, and CBS used to analyze Nielsen data.
In December of 2023, I left Lake 5 and made a pivot to freelance work in Audiovisual Technology, merging my joint passions for music and technology. During my time freelancing, I provided outstanding AV support to various companies in the NYC area including Five Star AV, the Maxus Group, and Southridge AV Productions, and worked a diverse array of events including corporate events, conferences, weddings, and bar mitzvahs.
Then in October 2024, I began a position as an IT technician at the luxury skin care company At Eve Lom, I provided crucial IT support to employees, including setting up new laptops for employees, managing Microsoft 365 logins, wiping laptops from old employees, troubleshooting issues with software and hardware, and authoring detailed spreadsheets on forecast data in Microsoft Excel. I worked at Eve Lom until July of 2026, when the US offices shut down.
Since October of 2025, I have been working part-time as an AV technician at the Liberty Science Center, where I provide crucial AV support at events as well as troubleshoot AV issues throughout the museum. I also provide IT support when needed, including troubleshooting issues with computers, networking and Wake on LAN.
With regards to programming, I am an AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and have extensive experience with several programming languages including C++, Java, JavaScript, HTML, and SQL, as well as a working knowledge of several others, including C# and Haskell. I have a BA in Computer Science from Bard College. You can find a compilation of my various programming projects by navigating to the "Portfolio" section of this website.
With regards to music, I play in an alternative rock band called "Retracer" and make ambient/experimental music under the moniker "World's Museum."
I hope you have fun exploring my website!
